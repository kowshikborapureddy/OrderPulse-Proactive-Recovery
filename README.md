# OrderPulse — Proactive Food Delivery Recovery

> **When delivery fails, recover the meal — not just the money.**

OrderPulse is a product concept for a proactive recovery layer in food delivery.

Instead of waiting until an order has already failed, OrderPulse explores how meaningful delivery risk can be detected earlier, explained clearly, and followed by realistic recovery options while the customer still has time to act.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Late-night food delivery can become difficult when ETAs change, preparation is delayed, tracking stops progressing, or delivery problems appear.

The core problem is not simply a late order.

**Customers may discover the problem too late to recover the meal.**

OrderPulse explores how recovery can begin earlier, while meaningful options may still exist.

---

## 💡 Solution

OrderPulse follows a simple proactive recovery flow:

**Monitor → Detect Risk → Warn → Explain → Offer Options → Customer Choice → Recover**

The experience helps customers understand what is happening and choose an available recovery path before the recovery window closes.

---

## 👤 Target User

The primary user is a customer ordering food during a time when alternatives may be limited, especially late at night.

They want:

- A reliable delivery experience
- Clear and accurate information
- Early warning when something goes wrong
- Realistic recovery options
- Control over the final decision

---

## 🚀 MVP

The MVP focuses on:

- Delivery-risk monitoring
- Early warning
- Risk explanation
- Eligibility and policy checks
- Recovery options
- Recovery recommendation
- Customer confirmation
- Outcome tracking
- Basic product metrics

### Out of Scope

- Fully automated cancellation
- Guaranteed replacement delivery
- Complex personalization
- Full operational control center
- Production integration with a real delivery platform

---

## 🤖 AI Product Approach

OrderPulse uses AI as an assistive layer.

AI can help:

- Summarize the delivery situation
- Explain why a warning was triggered
- Compare eligible recovery options
- Recommend an appropriate recovery path
- Generate concise customer communication

### Product Principle

**Rules decide eligibility.  
AI assists explanation and recommendation.  
Customer decides.**

AI does not independently determine refund, cancellation, or recovery eligibility.

---

## 📊 Product Metrics

Key metrics include:

- Successful Meal Recovery Rate
- Early Warning Rate
- Failed Order Rate
- Support Contact Rate
- Time to Resolution
- Customer Satisfaction

The product can be evaluated by comparing the proactive recovery experience against a traditional tracking/support journey.
